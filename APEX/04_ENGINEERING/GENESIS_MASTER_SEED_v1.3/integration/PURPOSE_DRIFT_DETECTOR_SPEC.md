# Purpose-Drift Detector v0.1

Flags modules that lack or weaken:

- real job;
- human benefit;
- whole-system usefulness;
- evidence;
- scope;
- risk review;
- tests;
- approval gates.

## Decision Outputs

- `NO_DRIFT`
- `WATCH`
- `REPAIR`
- `BLOCK`

## Boundary

The detector is advisory and gate-supporting until integrated with reviewed CI enforcement.
