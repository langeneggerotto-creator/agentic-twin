# T.A.S.T.E. Console Integration Plan

Add Purpose Gate status as a visible scoring section inside the T.A.S.T.E. Console.

## New Fields

- purpose gate decision;
- purpose MSE;
- drift score;
- blocker count;
- human approval required;
- truth boundary.

## Boundary

The console may display and score readiness, but it may not approve merge, production release, or physical action.
