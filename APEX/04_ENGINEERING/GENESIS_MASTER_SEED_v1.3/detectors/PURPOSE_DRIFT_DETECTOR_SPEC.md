# PURPOSE_DRIFT_DETECTOR_SPEC v0.1

## Purpose

Detect modules that look impressive but drift away from the Genesis Master Seed.

## Drift Signals

- missing real job
- missing human benefit
- missing whole-system usefulness
- weak evidence
- overstated maturity
- missing scope boundary
- missing dignity/safety review
- high visual complexity with low testability
- production-ready language without evidence

## Output

`PURPOSE_DRIFT_REPORT.md`

## Decisions

`NO_DRIFT / MINOR_DRIFT / REPAIR_REQUIRED / BLOCK / RESEED`
