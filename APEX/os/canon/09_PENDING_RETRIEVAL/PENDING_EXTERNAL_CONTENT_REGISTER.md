# Pending External Content Retrieval Register

## Status

External shortcut/link references supplied during OS ingestion are registered as pending references only. Their underlying content was not grounded during ingestion and is therefore **not semantically incorporated** into the OS.

## Governing Rule

```text
A pointer proves a reference exists.
It does not prove what the referenced content teaches.
```

Content may be incorporated only after it can be opened, supplied as a file/transcript/screenshot, or otherwise captured and reviewed as grounded source material.

## Pending Source Classes

| Source Class | Current Status | Next Proof Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Uploaded shortcut/reference pointers | `REGISTERED_PENDING_CONTENT_RETRIEVAL` | Open or supply grounded content for review |
| Previously supplied YouTube Short reference | `REGISTERED_PENDING_CONTENT_RETRIEVAL` | Supply accessible frames/transcript/video or retrieve content successfully |

## Automatic GitHub Capture Treatment

This register may be captured automatically as a safe truth-boundary document. It does not authorize automatic download, scraping, semantic adoption or republication of referenced external material.
